PadelCred privacy

PadelCred has no ads or tracking, and accounts are optional. Here's exactly what it stores and where.

What stays on your phone

Your scores, results, players, league, achievements, profile and friends list are saved inside the app on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting the app or clearing your browser's data removes it.

Videos and photos

The camera and microphone are only used while you're filming. Videos and clips stay in the app until you save or delete them. When you save, a copy goes to your phone's own storage. Your profile photo is saved on your phone only.

What you choose to share

If you don't sign in, nothing leaves your phone unless you share it yourself, for example a share card or a video clip.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you do, your results, profile and friends list are saved to your PadelCred account on Google Firebase, stored in London, so they sync between your devices.

Your profile is public to other signed-in PadelCred users. That means your name, username, photo, bio, padel details, socials, playing style, stats, achievements, Overall, ranking and your last 10 results (score, date, venue, and who you played with and against). Matches you play with connected friends are also sent to them, and matches tagged with a shared group are shared with its members, along with live scores while you play.

Your email address is never shown to anyone. It's only used to sign you in.

You can hide yourself from the rankings in Your data, and delete your account and everything in it at any time from Account and sync.

Other services the app uses

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services can see your device's IP address when the app loads. They don't receive any of your scores, videos or profile.
