PadelCred privacy

PadelCred has no ads, no tracking and never sells your data. Accounts are optional. Here's exactly what it stores, where, and who can see it.

Who runs PadelCred

PadelCred is made and run by Raza Rebuilt in the UK, who is responsible for your data. Questions or requests: padelcred@gmail.com.

If you don't sign in

Your scores, results, players, league, achievements, profile, plans and challenges are saved inside the app on your phone only. Nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app or clearing your browser's data removes it.

Videos and clips

The camera and microphone are only used while you're filming. Videos and clips stay on your phone, in the app, until you save, share or delete them. They are never uploaded to PadelCred.

If you create an account

Your account is stored on Google Firebase, in London, so it syncs between your devices and survives a new phone. It holds your email address (only used to sign you in, never shown to anyone), your profile (name, username, photo, bio, padel details, socials and archetype), your results, friends, shared groups, Americano nights, knockouts, match plans and rivalry challenges, and the ratings you give and receive.

What other players can see

Your profile is visible to other signed-in PadelCred players: your name, username, photo, bio, padel details, socials, archetype, stats, achievements, Overall, Skill radar, ranking and your last 10 results (score, date, venue and who played). Matches you play with friends are shared with them, and matches tagged with a shared group are shared with its members, along with live scores while you play.

Ratings are private: friends only ever see an average, never who gave which rating. The skills friends pick for you only appear combined, in your Skill radar.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile at any time, hide yourself from the rankings in Your data, export your results to a spreadsheet, and delete your account and everything in it from Account and sync. You also have the right to ask what we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted: email us and we'll respond within a month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. Reports you send us (bugs or profile reports) are kept until they're dealt with, then deleted.

Services the app uses

Google Firebase stores account data and handles sign-in. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address when the app loads. None of them receive your scores, videos or profile for their own use.

Why we use your data

Only to provide the features you choose to use: keeping your results, syncing your account, and showing your profile and matches to the players you play with.

Age

You need to be 13 or over to create an account.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app.
