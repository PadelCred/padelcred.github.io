The short version: be fair, be respectful, and enjoy your padel. Here are the rules for using PadelCred.

Using PadelCred

PadelCred is free to use. You need to be 13 or over to create an account. By creating one, you agree to these terms and the privacy policy.

Your account

Keep your sign-in details to yourself. One account per person, using a name that's genuinely yours. You're responsible for what happens on your account.

What you add

Your profile, photo, names, results and ratings must be honest and respectful. Don't upload anything offensive, pretend to be someone else, rate people unfairly or add results that didn't happen. You own what you add, and you give PadelCred permission to store it and show it in the app as described in the privacy policy.

Filming

If you film a match, make sure the other players are happy to be filmed, especially before sharing a clip.

Reports and removal

You can report a profile that breaks these rules. We may remove content, or suspend or delete accounts that break them.

Ratings and rankings

Overall ratings, predictions, rankings and badges are worked out from results and friends' ratings. They're for fun and motivation, not an official grading.
